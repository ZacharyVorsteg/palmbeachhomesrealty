Boynton Beach sits midway between West Palm Beach and Boca Raton on Florida's southeast coast, offering buyers a wide range of homes — from under-$200,000 condos in country club communities to $5 million-plus waterfront estates — all within Palm Beach County's school district and minutes from the Atlantic. According to Houzeo ([2026 market data](https://www.houzeo.com/housing-market/florida/boynton-beach)), the median sale price is $415,000, with roughly 1,590 active listings across single-family homes, condos, and townhomes. This guide covers what to expect from the market, which communities fit which buyer profiles, the schools that serve each part of the city, and the practical steps involved in purchasing here.

## What the Boynton Beach Market Looks Like in 2026

According to Houzeo ([2026 market data](https://www.houzeo.com/housing-market/florida/boynton-beach)), the median home price in Boynton Beach is $415,000, representing a 4.01% increase from 2025. That positions Boynton Beach below the price pressure of coastal towns like Boca Raton and Palm Beach while reflecting steady demand from retirees, families, and remote workers relocating from the Northeast and Midwest.

According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), there are 1,592 active residential listings in Boynton Beach across all price points. The city covers 16.71 square miles, stretching from the barrier island on the east to the edge of the Everglades on the west, with a population of approximately 80,000 residents.

Homes spend varying time on market depending on price tier and community type. Waterfront and estate properties in the $1 million-plus range often sit longer — a 5,283 square foot home on S Lake Drive was active for 20 days at $3.995 million — while entry-level condos in the $200,000–$300,000 range typically see more competition (Bex Realty, [2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)).

## Property Types Available in Boynton Beach

According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), the Boynton Beach residential market breaks into four primary property categories, each serving a different buyer profile.

**Single-family homes** are the most common type and span the widest price range — from $265,000 for a small home in a non-gated neighborhood to $5.2 million for a lakefront estate. Most mid-range single-family inventory in the $350,000–$700,000 band sits in gated communities with HOA fees and shared amenities.

**Condos** represent the most accessible entry point, with some listings below $200,000. Country club condos in communities like Hunters Run are the primary source of sub-$200,000 inventory in 2026. Buyers in this category should account for HOA fees, mandatory club membership costs, and the association's reserve funding status before finalizing an offer.

**Townhomes** fall between condos and single-family homes in both price and maintenance commitment. Communities like Limetree offer townhome inventory in the $260,000–$280,000 range without age restrictions, which widens the buyer pool and tends to support resale values over time.

**Country clubs and active adult communities** operate under their own HOA and membership structures. According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), Boynton Beach's main categories in this segment are Aberdeen Country Club, Hunters Run, and Quail Ridge for country clubs, and Cascades, Palm Isles, Ponte Vecchio, and Indian Spring for the 55+ market.

## Country Club Communities

According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), Boynton Beach has three primary country club communities where a home purchase typically includes access — or the option of access — to a private golf course, tennis facilities, swimming pools, and club dining: Aberdeen Country Club, Hunters Run, and Quail Ridge.

**Aberdeen Country Club** is a gated community in west Boynton Beach with golf, tennis, and a full social calendar. Homes range from two-bedroom condos to larger single-family residences. The club offers tiered membership with social-only options for buyers who do not golf, which reduces the mandatory cost compared to full golf membership.

**Hunters Run** offers a similar country club profile with waterfront condo units available below $200,000, making it one of the more accessible entry points into a gated lifestyle in South Florida (Bex Realty, [2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)). Mandatory membership fees and initiation costs apply — buyers should request the current fee schedule and add it to their monthly cost calculation before comparing this against non-club alternatives.

**Quail Ridge** occupies a larger footprint west of Congress Avenue, with single-family homes on golf course lots. Per Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), pricing here competes with similar clubs in neighboring Delray Beach while maintaining comparable facility quality — an advantage for buyers who want the club lifestyle at a relative discount.

Before purchasing in any country club community, obtain the club's current initiation fee schedule, the monthly dues statement, and the governing documents covering membership transfer rules. These costs are not always disclosed at the listing level and can add $500–$1,500 or more per month to actual housing cost.

## Active Adult (55+) Communities

According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), Boynton Beach has substantial inventory of age-restricted communities built to serve retirees and pre-retirees who want an active social environment without mandatory golf membership costs. Many of these communities are located west of Jog Road and priced well below $800,000 for single-family homes.

**Cascades and Cascade Lakes** are two related communities sharing a brand name but operating under separate HOA entities. Both offer resort-style amenities — pickleball, tennis, heated pools, fitness centers — at HOA fees generally lower than what country clubs charge. The distinction between the two communities matters for resale and HOA rules, so buyers should clarify which entity governs a specific address before making an offer.

**Palm Isles** is a gated 55+ community where current listings are around $420,000 for three-bedroom homes exceeding 2,600 square feet (Bex Realty, [2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)). The size-to-price ratio draws buyers who want space at a cost that would buy something significantly smaller in Boca Raton or Delray Beach.

**Ponte Vecchio** and **Indian Spring** round out the active adult category. Indian Spring is built on a golf course but does not require mandatory membership — removing the largest recurring expense buyers in country club purchases typically encounter. This makes it a practical hybrid for buyers who want the golf course setting without the club financial commitment.

If you are purchasing a condo in a 55+ building, Florida law now mandates structural integrity reserve studies and reserve funding requirements following the post-Surfside legislation. Associations that are behind on reserve funding may face special assessments, and lenders increasingly scrutinize this before approving financing. The [Buying a Condo in Palm Beach County guide](https://palmbeachhomesrealty.com/blog/buying-a-condo-in-palm-beach-county-2026/) covers the milestone inspection and reserve rules in detail.

This segment is led by Equus, Golf Village, and Boynton Waters, with entry prices generally above $1 million and current listings extending to $5.2 million.

**Equus** is designed for equestrian buyers, with large acreage lots that accommodate horses, riding trails, and barn structures under the community's governing rules. It is one of the few communities in Palm Beach County where a buyer can legally stable horses within a gated residential community — a specific use case that commands a premium in a region where large agricultural lots are rare.

**Golf Village** offers new construction through the $3.5 million price point, with large lots, custom pool configurations, and waterfront positions on private lakes (Bex Realty, [2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)). **Boynton Waters** (old and new phases) attracts buyers who want lake or canal access without country club overhead. Per Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), pricing here represents a discount per square foot compared to waterfront properties in Delray Beach and Boca Raton.

Waterfront purchases in any part of Boynton Beach require a review of the property's FEMA flood zone designation. Properties near the Intracoastal, coastal canals, and low-lying inland areas may carry mandatory flood insurance requirements. ## Condos and Townhomes

For buyers who want lower entry prices or reduced maintenance responsibility, Boynton Beach has a substantial condo and townhome inventory. According to Bex Realty ([2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), the city has active condo listings from under $200,000 in older country club buildings to over $1 million for newer construction with Intracoastal or ocean-proximity views.

Townhome communities like Limetree offer two-bedroom units under $270,000 in a non-age-restricted setting — unusual at that price point in South Florida. These typically include community pools but no golf or club amenities, keeping HOA fees more predictable and the buyer pool broader on resale.

The most important due diligence step for any condo purchase in 2026 is verifying the association's reserve funding status. Underfunded reserves translate directly into risk of special assessments and complicate financing approvals from lenders who now screen for this before committing to a loan.

## Buying a Home in Boynton Beach: What to Expect

Boynton Beach transactions follow Palm Beach County's standard title and closing process. Most closings occur 30 to 45 days after a ratified contract, though cash purchases in the estate segment often close faster. The Palm Beach County Property Appraiser's Office ([pbcpao.gov](https://www.pbcpao.gov/)) records all deed transfers publicly and is the authoritative source for assessed values, ownership history, and exemption status.

For buyers financing a purchase, pre-approval from a Florida-licensed lender is standard before listing agents schedule showings on high-demand properties. In competitive price brackets — particularly the $350,000–$550,000 range — sellers regularly receive multiple offers within days of a listing going active.

**Open house schedule.** Open houses in Boynton Beach typically run on weekend afternoons, with some communities hosting private previews for registered buyers. The agent at an open house represents the seller — buyers can attend freely but should not share negotiating information or their motivation level with the seller's agent.

**Homestead exemption timing.** Homestead exemption eligibility starts January 1 of the year after your purchase closes, as long as the property is your primary residence on that date. The filing deadline with the Palm Beach County Property Appraiser ([pbcpao.gov](https://www.pbcpao.gov/)) is March 1 of the application year. See the [Palm Beach County Homestead Exemption Guide](https://palmbeachhomesrealty.com/blog/palm-beach-county-property-tax-exemptions-homestead-2026/) for the full process and the Save Our Homes cap that limits annual assessment increases once you are established.

**Inventory context.** With 1,592 active listings in the market (Bex Realty, [2026](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), buyers have more inventory to consider than during the peak 2021–2022 cycle. Sellers in the mid-range segment are more willing to negotiate on price or cover closing costs than two years ago, though well-priced listings in desirable gated communities still attract competitive offers within the first week.

## Palm Beach County Schools Serving Boynton Beach

Boynton Beach is served by the Palm Beach County School District ([palmbeachschools.org](https://www.palmbeachschools.org/)), one of the ten largest public school districts in the United States. School assignments are zone-based, meaning a home's specific address — not the city name or neighborhood marketing — determines which schools a child attends.

**Boynton Beach Community High School** serves grades 9–12 within city limits. According to the National Center for Education Statistics ([NCES School ID 120150003755](https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/schoolsearch/school_detail.asp?Search=1&ID=120150003755)), the school is part of the Palm Beach County district, with publicly accessible enrollment and grade configuration data.

Elementary and middle school assignments vary by address within the city. Buyers with school-age children should verify zone assignments directly with the Palm Beach County School District's ([palmbeachschools.org](https://www.palmbeachschools.org/schools/high-schools)) online zone-lookup tool rather than relying on real estate marketing materials, community websites, or agent descriptions — attendance boundaries can change when the district rezones, and outdated information is common on third-party sites.

West Boynton Beach communities that sit near the Wellington boundary may draw from different zone assignments than east Boynton addresses closer to the coast. The specific community address determines the zone, not the community name.

Private school alternatives in the surrounding area include options in Delray Beach and Boca Raton, both within a reasonable commute from most Boynton Beach neighborhoods depending on traffic on US-1, I-95, and the Florida Turnpike.

## Life in Boynton Beach

Boynton Beach describes itself as "the gateway to the gulf stream," reflecting its position where the warm Atlantic current runs unusually close to shore — a factor that supports the city's sport fishing and boating culture, and continues to draw buyers who want Intracoastal and ocean access without Palm Beach or Delray Beach price premiums.

The city's commercial infrastructure is well-developed. Shopping centers include Renaissance Commons, Boynton Beach Town Center, Boynton Beach Mall, Oakwood Square, and Boynton Commons (Bex Realty, [2026 listings](https://www.bexrealty.com/real-estate/Boynton-Beach/)), covering grocery, dining, medical, and retail needs without requiring drives to neighboring cities.

Downtown Boynton Beach, centered on Ocean Avenue near the Intracoastal, is undergoing active redevelopment. According to The Coastal Star ([thecoastalstar.com](https://thecoastalstar.com/profiles/blogs/boynton-seeks-wave-of-projects-to-build-up-downtown-waterfront)), the city is seeking $30 million in funding to advance waterfront and downtown development projects. The City of Boynton Beach ([boynton-beach.org](https://www.boynton-beach.org/164/Public-Arts)) reports that its Arts & Cultural Center, renovated in 2018, has hosted rotating visual arts and performing arts exhibitions since 2020.

For outdoor recreation, Boynton Beach Oceanfront Park on the barrier island is a public beach park with metered parking and direct Atlantic access. The Arthur R. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge forms the city's western boundary, offering kayaking, birding, and nature trails on over 140,000 acres of Everglades wetlands.

## What to Know About HOAs and Carrying Costs

Almost every gated or amenitized community in Boynton Beach carries an HOA. Monthly costs range widely — from under $100 for gate-only communities without amenities to over $1,500 per month when a mandatory country club membership is included. These fees are counted in your debt-to-income ratio when lenders qualify you, so they directly affect how much home you can finance.

Beyond the HOA, Florida buyers carry costs that differ from most other states:

**Property insurance.** Florida's property insurance market has seen insurer exits and premium increases since 2022. Citizens Property Insurance Corporation remains the state's insurer of last resort. Premiums vary based on flood zone, roof age, construction year, and wind mitigation rating. Getting an insurance quote before making an offer — not after — prevents surprises at closing.

**Roof age.** Many Florida carriers decline to write or renew a policy when the roof exceeds 15 years. A roof approaching that threshold is a common source of seller-to-buyer negotiation in the final inspection period. Buyers should request the roof permit and inspection history through the county's permit lookup before waiving inspection rights.

**Property taxes.** Boynton Beach properties are assessed by the Palm Beach County Property Appraiser ([pbcpao.gov](https://www.pbcpao.gov/)). New buyers pay taxes based on the assessed value in the purchase year. Homestead exemption, once established, limits future increases through the Save Our Homes cap — but it does not apply to the purchase year or the year immediately following if you miss the March 1 filing deadline.
