## General CRE Blog Best Practices (Baseline Until Data Exists)

### Content Structure
- **2,000–3,500 words** for pillar content (shorter for news/updates)
- Lead with a "Key Facts" or "TL;DR" box — real estate searchers want numbers fast
- Include 2–4 comparison tables (cost comparisons, program eligibility, etc.)
- End every article with 5–7 FAQ questions using H3 headers
- Cite 8+ named sources with dates — YMYL content gets scrutinized

### Title Formats That Rank for CRE
Ranked by typical search performance:
1. **Data-driven + year**: "Palm Beach County [Topic]: [Specific Number] ([Year] Guide)"
2. **How-to**: "How to [Action] in Palm Beach County ([Year])"
3. **Question**: "[Question]? What Palm Beach County [Audience] Need to Know"
4. **Comparison**: "[Option A] vs [Option B] in Palm Beach County: [Year] Breakdown"

### Meta Description Pattern
- 150–160 characters
- Lead with the trigger/urgency: regulatory change, market shift, program launch
- Include one specific number or date
- End with value prop: "here's what you need to know" / "complete breakdown"

### Keyword Strategy
- Every article targets 1 primary long-tail keyword (4–7 words)
- Include 3–5 secondary keywords naturally in H2/H3 headers
- Geo-modify everything: "palm beach county" > "south florida" > "florida"
- Year-tag titles: "(2026 Guide)" signals freshness to Google

### Internal Linking Rules (As Content Grows)
- Every new article links to 2–3 existing articles
- Update existing articles to link to new ones (cross-pollination)
- Use descriptive anchor text, not "click here"
- Link from high-traffic pages to new content (once analytics shows which pages those are)
